# VoxEx Roadmap

What's coming to VoxEx, in build order. This is a living document — features ship when they're ready, and details may change as they're built.

---

# 1. Survival Mode

VoxEx grows from a creative sandbox into a game that can hurt you.

- **Game modes** — choose Survival or Creative when creating a world, and switch later if you want; your sandbox worlds stay sandbox
- **Health & damage** — fall damage, mob attacks, death and respawn
- **Starvation** — you'll need to find food and eat
- **Sleep & exhaustion** — stay awake too long and it takes a toll; after 3 days without sleep, you'll start to hallucinate
- **Beds** — craft one, sleep through the night, wake rested with your respawn point set
- **Temperature** — extreme heat burns, extreme cold freezes; dress for the biome you're exploring

# 2. Smarter Creatures

A new shared behavior system gives every mob a real personality — pursuing, fleeing, grazing, perching, ambushing — with proper navigation instead of walking into walls.

- **New mobs, added one at a time, fully finished** — model, sounds, behavior, and drops complete before the next one starts. Planned: owls, snakes, deer, squirrels, and more
- **Smarter zombies** — better senses, better pursuit
- Every creature reacts to you: deer bolt, owls take flight if you approach too loudly, snakes wait

# 3. A Richer World

- **Block variants** — new log, leaf, and plank types; different stones; both generated in the world and craftable
- **Better trees** — more species, realistic branching shapes, and physical behavior: break the bottom log and the tree comes down; leaves wither when cut off from their trunk
- **New biomes** — Desert, Tundra, and Badlands (each one a real threat once temperature damage arrives)
- **Ores & the underground** — caves stop being empty: common metals for tool and gear tiers, and rare deep crystals that fuel high-power magic
- **Food** — hunt game for meat first, then forage berries and wild edibles, then farm crops, then fish the rivers and lakes
- **Weather** — rain that douses fires, blizzards that will freeze you without shelter, lightning that can start them, snow that blankets the world. Weather is a survival force, not a backdrop
- **Crafting basics** — turn what you gather into what you need

# 4. Magic, Earned

Magic stops being free and starts being a skill.

- **Spells damage enemies** — magic becomes a real combat option
- **Casting costs** — a mana reserve for everyday casts, rare reagents for the most powerful ones
- **Mastery** — the more you use a spell, the better you get: stronger effects, lower costs. Reagent costs keep spam-leveling in check — cast with intent
- **Ruins & lost places** — generated towers, shrines, and abandoned camps scattered through the world. Some hold spell tomes, altars, and reagent caches — the path to new magic. Others hold food, materials, and a bed; stumbling onto a ruined campsite in a blizzard might save your life

# Later Ideas

- More biomes: Jungle, Shaded Forest (canopy so thick the floor stays dark at noon), Autumn/Cherry forests
- Expanded sound design: footsteps per surface, mob voices, ambient dread when something is hunting you
- Mounted combat (jousting?)

# Recently Shipped

- **Magic System** — M toggles magic mode: Explosion, Laser, Freeze, and Fireball spells with 5 power levels, channeled casting, and terrain destruction
- **Mobile / Touch Controls** — full touch parity: joystick movement, gesture mining/placing, on-screen actions
- **Longwoods Biome** — giant multi-trunk trees, 12-24 blocks tall
- **Inventory & Hotbar** — drag-and-drop inventory (E)
